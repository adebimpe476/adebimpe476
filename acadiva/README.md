## How to review

Read the documents in numerical order. The master blueprint is the starting point; specialist manuals contain detailed requirements and decisions. Distinguish confirmed decisions from proposals, assumptions and unresolved questions. Do not assume a feature is implemented merely because it appears in the specification.

## Independent AI / expert review brief

Review this entire folder as a critical product, education, technology, security, UX, business and go-to-market reviewer. Return:

1. Your understanding of ACADIVA in one paragraph.
2. What is genuinely differentiated and what is commodity functionality.
3. Missing users, workflows, requirements or risks.
4. Contradictions, overengineering and unnecessary complexity.
5. The strongest MVP for a Nigerian private-school pilot.
6. Architecture, data-protection, child-safety, tenant-isolation and security concerns.
7. Practical pricing, onboarding, support and adoption risks for Nigerian schools.
8. Design and brand direction, including what parents, teachers and proprietors should immediately understand.
9. A prioritised 30/60/90-day execution plan.
10. The five most important questions the founder must answer.

Be direct. Cite the exact document and section for every significant criticism. Separate evidence from assumptions. Do not invent market research or claim to have tested the product. Recommend changes, but do not rewrite the source of truth without explaining why.

## Feedback protocol

Submit review comments as a GitHub issue or pull request, or provide a separate review document. Preserve the original product documents. Proposed changes must be labelled as proposals until accepted by the founder.
